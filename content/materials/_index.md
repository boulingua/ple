Every unit of this course is accompanied by classroom materials, generated from
the branded LaTeX templates (`slidegen` and `sheetgen`) and **committed** to the
repository rather than built on deployment. They are therefore versioned with
the prose they belong to, and a given PDF always corresponds to the unit page as
it stood when the PDF was made.

- **Slide decks** — one deck per unit, following the five-move sequence, issued
  as an editable **.odp** alongside the PDF so that a teacher can cut, reorder or
  translate without asking permission.
- **Worksheets** — one printable A4 worksheet per unit, with a separate answer
  key, laid out for photocopying in greyscale.
- **Exam PDFs** — the printable form of each unit's sibling exam, filed under
  `static/downloads/<level>/`.
- **Audio** — native European Portuguese voice for vocabulary, dialogues and
  reading texts, produced through the shared `audiogen` workflow and linked from
  the unit pages themselves rather than listed here.
